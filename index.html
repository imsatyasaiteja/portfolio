About Me

I have practical experience with security tools and workflows for inspecting HTTP requests, monitoring events, detecting suspicious traffic patterns, and designing SIEM-style dashboards for investigation and analysis.

Applied Security Across the Stack

During my time at NUS, I focused on applying security principles across the stack, connecting systems and network security with secure database storage techniques and cloud infrastructure. This experience changed my perspective on security, helping me see it as a deeper layer of software and systems design and understand how various layers of the stack interact and can be secured together.

Mission-Critical Engineering at the Aeronautical Test Range

As a Junior Research Fellow at the Defence R&D Organization (DRDO), Govt. of India, I worked on telemetry communication between Tapas and Nishant reconnaissance UAVs and the Ground Control Station. This involved configuring actuator parameters, troubleshooting issues, and monitoring communication during live flight trials at the Aeronautical Test Range. The experience shaped my understanding of how mission-critical systems need to be monitored, maintained, and documented to remain reliable in operational critical environments.
